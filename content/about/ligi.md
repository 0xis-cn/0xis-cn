<p style="text-align: center">
<span style="font-size: 1.75em">Selected works</span><br><i>of</i><br>
<span style="font-variant: small-caps; font-size: 2.5em">Ligi Studio</span>
</p>

Numerous sinophonic writers adopt a pseudonym ([*hào*, *gō*, *ho*, or *tên hiệu*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_name)) according to their residence or workplace. Liang Keetyau, as a random example, named himself that redundantly translated to „the host of ice-drinker’s studio“.

In [Muqliong Gang](@/blog/2022-05-14-childhood.md), the spiritual homeland of my childhood, I created the Gang-affiliated Ligi Studio around 877 DM. It and Mocsieng Workshop was institutionalised as late Muqliong’s outreach for education, research, and make-believe.

We children were vulnerable in defensing our fantasy. Writing down these words, however, I realised that I have done everything possible. Two-years-worth works are not idle daydreams. *Zienndzaehienzhag*, a fiction-themed application (it *was installable*) and one another. Filming and editing of late Muqliong DVs. Visual design and maintenance of a livestream. Failed mechanic-fashional prototype laid in the bungalow. Various works for administrators.

Conclusion: My later, ordinary self must have been cursed Original Sin…

I dare not boast to be luckier than those who once claimed to be god-descendants, since I still have a shot at catching up with my imagination. At the age Hu Shih abandoned his workplace name, no longer „shielding [his] brilliance“, I do exactly the opposite. Here are the works I believe still deserve the honour of bearing Ligi Studio’s name.

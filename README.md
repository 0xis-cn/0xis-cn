## Using GitHub under protest

[![Please don't upload to GitHub](https://nogithub.codeberg.page/badge.svg)](https://nogithub.codeberg.page)

I am moving my projects away from GitHub, a hypocritical FOSS supporter and a bad place for software development. I invite you to read the [Give up GitHub](https://GiveUpGitHub.org) campaign or [a concise essay](https://ploum.net/2023-02-22-leaving-github.html) by Ploum. I will leave my social image on GitHub since that is the expected use case of its non-trivial features.

> Developers are now raw meat encouraged to get stars, followers and commit counters, doing the most stupid things in the most appealing way to get... visibility! Yeah! Engagement! Followers! Audience!    
> — Ploum

For projects I developed alone, you are welcomed to email me or even [send patches](https://git-send-email.io/).

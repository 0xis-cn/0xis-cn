This page is a performative list for languages that I speak (or <ml-n>speak not</ml-n>). You might be uninterested in [my linguistic history](@/blog/2025-05-26-my-linguistic-history.md). As you may infer, I love languages for some extent (though I used to see handling more languages unnecessary), and was once a firebrand on language issues. I am a lot more tender now, with an exception to disparage those [deeming most culture as language-dependent](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/502816847) or *flex the muscle* of some languages. Isn’t it only a small portion of dedicated culture like Klingon that is language-dependent?
